## Characterization limitations (must be disclosed in every report)

1. Liberty is the **`--skip-characterization` estimated model** — constant 0.05 ns setup/hold, no slew/capacitance tables of real silicon, no `ff` sequential groups, no power. It is *not* a characterized timing model.
2. No behavioral Verilog model → **no RTL simulation possible** for the macro; protocol correctness of the controller cannot be sim-verified locally without writing a labeled mock model (follow-up, Verification).
3. No LVS/PEX (Calibre absent, D-004) → layout-vs-schematic correctness of the macro is *assumed from generator*, not independently verified here.
4. Dimensions are ASAP7 **1x scale** (matches `asap7_tech_1x_*.lef` in ORFS).

## Compatibility facts used by the benchmark

- All signal pins on M3/M4 → reachable by router; OBS M1–M5 means PDN must connect `vdd`/`vss` (M3) to the platform grid strategy (M1-M2-M5-M6) — **watch item for Phase B/C**.
- `SYMMETRY X Y` → placer may flip/rotate; keep `MACRO_PLACE_HALO ≥ 2 µm` (riscv32i uses 4 4).
- Cell is `CLASS BLOCK` → place with macro placement flow (not standard-cell rows).
