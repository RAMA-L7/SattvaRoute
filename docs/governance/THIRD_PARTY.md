## Benchmark package note

The published reproducibility benchmark `benchmark/design/nangate_macro_control/` uses the Nangate45 platform and its `fakeram45_64x32` abstract macro. Those platform files are **external and pinned by hash**, not included. Earlier `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` listed ORFS/OpenFinRAM/ASAP7/Yosys but did not call out the Nangate45/FakeRAM package; `docs/governance/LICENSING.md` and this inventory now document it. The root notice is supplemented rather than rewritten.
