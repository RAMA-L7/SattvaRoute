# Goals

Separated. Not all are equally mature; see `docs/reference/CAPABILITIES.md`.

## Engineering goals
- Evidence-driven diagnosis; traceable claims; missing evidence stays missing.
- Narrow, correctly-scoped corrections; no broad deletion; no corner change; no constraint relaxation.
- Isolated recovery; preserve originals; invalidate downstream; stage-limited rerun; independent verification.
- Honest statuses; flow-completion separated from engineering acceptance; no signoff claim.

## Product goals
- Clear product identity; coherent professional docs; reproducible control benchmark; verified setup/launch; practical user journey; authoritative terminology; evidence matrix.

## Research goals
- Evidence-grounded EDA agent research; diagnosis/recovery evaluation; frozen rubric; held-out unseen case; no post-result tuning; justified abstention; not count live-AI without credentials.

## Safety goals
- Human control; explicit approval of exact diff; patch integrity; no fabrication; no credential exposure; no silent constraint/rule change; preserve history; stop on safety failure.

## Reproducibility goals
- Pinned ORFS + local patches; verified revision; collateral + tool checksums; manifest v2 + fingerprint; no author paths; no untracked reliance; no bulk artifacts; baseline preservation.

## Usability goals
- Reasonable defaults; documented bounded Make syntax; documented artifacts; troubleshooting; verified commands only. (Not claiming validated UX.)
