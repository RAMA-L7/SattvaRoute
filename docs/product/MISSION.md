# Mission

Provide a **reproducible, auditable, evidence-driven** way to diagnose physical-design failures and execute **narrowly scoped, explicitly approved** recovery, while keeping humans in control and preserving historical truth.

## What we do

- Capture immutable run evidence and artifacts; fingerprint inputs; verify pinned revisions/collateral/tool identity.
- Independently verify execution, artifacts, routing, timing, coverage, extraction, evidence; never turn missing data into success.
- Derive failure mechanism from evidence; propose precise, bounded, reviewable corrections; never invent collateral or relax constraints.
- Keep **deterministic checks**, **model-generated reasoning**, **human approval**, **patch application**, and **engineering verification** strictly separate.
- Preserve originals and history; recover in isolation; verify independently; report honestly.

## What we don't do

See `docs/product/NON_GOALS.md`. In short: not replace engineers, not autonomous production modification, not signoff, not EDA replacement, not general AI engineering competence.
